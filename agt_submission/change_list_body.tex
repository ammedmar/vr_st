\section*{Major changes}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Cohomology operations (\S3.1--3.3).}
\begin{itemize}
\item In \S3.1, added the assumption that $\pi$ is abelian also for $n=1$.
\item In \S3.3, corrected the description of the suspension isomorphism as a natural isomorphism, rather than a map induced by inclusion into the suspension.
\item Corrected the description of stable operations from a colimit to an inverse limit and specified its transition maps.
\item Corrected the definition of the graded algebra of stable operations by replacing the disjoint union with a direct sum.
\item In the projective- and lens-space examples, specified that cohomology is unreduced.
\end{itemize}

\item \textbf{Persistent operations (\S3.4).}
Explained why additive Steenrod operations over $\mathbb F_p$ are linear, citing Hatcher, \S4.L.

\item \textbf{Product formulas (\S3.5).}
\begin{itemize}
\item In the product lemma, added the hypothesis that at least one factor takes values in finite-type spaces.
\item In the lemma and the Vietoris--Rips product theorem, specified that the total Steenrod operation acts on unreduced cohomology.
\item Expanded the proof of the image formula using the Cartan formula and the image of a tensor product.
\item Added the justification for the kernel formula: the total operation is injective, so all three kernels are zero.
\item Clarified that the identifications are natural in the filtration parameter.
\end{itemize}

\item \textbf{Critical radii (\S4.1, 4.6).}
\begin{itemize}
\item In \S4.1.3, removed the incorrect assertion that filling radius is independent of the coefficient field.
\item In item (3) of the lemma in \S4.6, corrected the nonzero-image condition from $k\leq(n-1)/2$ to $m\leq n$, retaining the condition that $\binom{m-k}{k}$ is odd.
\item In the proof of item (3), made the zero-image cases explicit: $m>n$ or $\binom{m-k}{k}$ even.
\end{itemize}

\item \textbf{Lens spaces (\S4.7 and \S5.1).}
\begin{itemize}
\item In \S4.7, corrected the metric normalization by replacing the radius-$q$ sphere with the radius-$2$ sphere, giving the quotient diameter $\pi$ for $n\geq1$.
\item In the lemma, restricted the conclusion from all degrees $m\leq2n+1$ to odd degrees $1\leq m\leq2n+1$.
\item Extended the filling-radius monotonicity assumption to begin with $L_q^0$, rather than $L_q^1$.
\item In the proof, corrected the scaling factor from $1/q$ to $1/2$ and the starting index from $1$ to $0$.
\item In \S5.1, restricted the conditional verification of Desideratum~(2) to odd degrees and stated that the even-degree inequalities remain to be verified.
\end{itemize}

\item \textbf{Hypotheses for the distance estimates (\S5.1).}
\begin{itemize}
\item Added $\dim H_m(M)=1$ to Desideratum~(3).
\item In the upper-bound lemma (\S5.1.3), strengthened the hypothesis from $H_m(M)\neq0$ to $\dim H_m(M)=1$.
\item In the lower-bound lemma (\S5.1.4), added the hypothesis $\ell\neq m$.
\item For real projective spaces, replaced the verification of Desideratum~(3) by the explicit choice $\mathrm{Sq}^1:H^1\to H^2$ for $n>1$.
\item For lens spaces, corrected the choice of operation in Desideratum~(3) from a reduced power to the Bockstein $H^1\to H^2$ for $n\geq1$.
\item In both verifications, specified that the target homology is one-dimensional.
\end{itemize}

\item \textbf{Bounds and parameter ranges (\S5.2).}
\begin{itemize}
\item In item (1) of the theorem, corrected the first inequality from $<$ to $\leq$.
\item In item (2) of the theorem, corrected the parameter restrictions from $m\leq n$ and $k\leq(n-1)/2$ to $0<k\leq m\leq n$, retaining the condition that $\binom{m-k}{k}$ is odd.
\end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}
